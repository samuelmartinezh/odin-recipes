Guacamole

Why choose this recipe

This homemade guacamole made with ripe avocados and lime juice gets a tasty kick from cayenne and cilantro. You can serve it smooth or chunky depending on your tastes. Serve with tortilla chips.

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ avocados - peeled, pitted, and mashed
  • ½ lime, juiced
  • ½ teaspoon salt or to taste
  • 1 roma (plum) tomatoes, diced
  • ¼ cup diced onion
  • 1 ½ tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro
  • ½ teaspoon minced garlic
  • ½ pinch ground cayenne pepper (Optional)

How do you make it?

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Mash avocados, lime juice, and salt together in a medium bowl; mix in tomatoes, onion, cilantro, and garlic.
  3. Stir in cayenne pepper. Serve immediately, or cover and refrigerate for 1 hour for improved flavor.
